# Problem Statement: The Student Information Overload

## The Core Issue
College students face an overwhelming influx of digital information daily. Important academic updates, assignment deadlines, and highly competitive placement opportunities are frequently buried in crowded inboxes. 

## Key Pain Points
1. **Scattered Deadlines:** Assignments and reporting times are sent via email but require manual data entry into calendars or task managers, leading to missed deadlines.
2. **Opaque Attachments:** Crucial data regarding placement shortlists, lab groups, or exam seating is often locked inside unstructured PDFs or massive Excel sheets. Finding a specific student's Registration Number is manual and error-prone.
3. **Passive Systems:** Current email clients wait for the user to search for information. They do not proactively alert users to actionable events.
4. **Missed Career Opportunities:** Placement emails contain specific skill requirements. Students often fail to recognize their own skill gaps or forget to update their resumes for specific roles in time for the application deadline.
5. **Lack of Personalization:** Every student has a unique profile (Registration Number, Name, Major, Clubs). Generic filters cannot adapt to a student's evolving identity and preferences.

## The Consequence
Increased anxiety, missed career opportunities, and wasted time spent doing manual administrative work instead of focusing on learning and development.
